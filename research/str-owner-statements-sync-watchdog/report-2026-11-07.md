# Research report: Owner statements and channel-sync watchdog for STR managers

Idea `2a90717d-4f02-4cb6-9307-e36c88b9c9b4` · method v3 · 2026-11-07 · pack: `pack-2026-11-07.json`
Import: 107 verified, 0 unverifiable, 0 rejected. 11 competitors, 19 companies, 3 contacts.

## Bottom line

The pain is real but sits in the reconciliation gaps, not in the absence of owner statements. Managers describe double entry, manual money-in/money-out totals and Airbnb co-host split payouts that don't match the statement math [ev-02, ev-08, ev-09, ev-11]. Price drift between PriceLabs, the PMS and Airbnb/Vrbo is common and documented [ev-35, ev-38, ev-43, ev-47].

The main premise is weak, though. Hospitable now ships owner statements, co-host statements and invoices, owner payouts and split payouts [ev-12, ev-15, ev-17, ev-19]. Hostaway and OwnerRez have statements too [ev-20, ev-21]. OwnerCenter already sells an owner-statement add-on at the $49/month price point [ev-61]. The evidence is mostly forum and vendor material, so the case is moderate. The add-on has to beat "good enough" incumbent features.

## What supports the problem

- **Owner-statement workflows are painful in practice.** One OwnerRez user calls the trust model "a freaking nightmare from a practical POV" [ev-01]. It forces double manual entry [ev-02] and "a LOT of work with a great deal of chance for error" [ev-05], plus at least $80/month of QuickBooks [ev-04].
- **Even satisfied users have a gap.** A licensed manager with 30 condos must still work out the money-in vs. distributions total by hand [ev-06, ev-08].
- **Co-host split payouts break statements.** Hospitable's new co-host statements didn't match Airbnb's actual payouts for one co-host, who "had to revert back to the hacky workarounds" [ev-09]. Another reconciles in a spreadsheet outside Hospitable [ev-10], because Airbnb computes the split its own way [ev-11]. Owners can't see the co-host's Airbnb transaction history [ev-24]. Under co-hosting the co-host must invoice the owner separately [ev-27].
- **Manual payouts are common** (vendor-relayed trade press) [ev-14]. Reconciling PMS data against the ledger is paid, specialised work [ev-33].
- **Price drift is recurring.**
  - Vrbo calendar vs. quote differed by up to $1,300, and the host turned PriceLabs off [ev-35, ev-36].
  - Airbnb promotions silently stop the rate sync, and staff call it "a frequent question" [ev-38, ev-39].
  - Prices were not reflected at all [ev-40, ev-42], and whole 12-month batches can be rejected over a 10% jump [ev-43].
  - PriceLabs admits broadcast delays caused bad-price bookings [ev-47].
  - Guesty reviews mention Vrbo sync edge cases that need manual cleanup [ev-48].
- **Lock-code failures occur.**
  - A code mismatch locked a guest out [ev-54, ev-55].
  - Hospitable has no retry button for failed codes, and the feature request was rejected [ev-50, ev-51].
  - Backup codes confused guests [ev-52].
- **Co-hosting is growing.** Airbnb's network launched with more than 10,000 co-hosts, and 20,000 more expressed interest [ev-25, ev-26].

## What argues against it

- **Incumbents already cover owner statements.**
  - Hospitable has one-click statements and a portal [ev-17], native owner payouts (Mogul plan) [ev-15, ev-16], split payouts [ev-19], and co-host statements and invoices [ev-12].
  - Hostaway [ev-20] and OwnerRez [ev-21] have statements too. A 30-unit manager says OwnerRez reports cover most of what she needs [ev-07].
  - "Hospitable could add owner accounting" is no longer a risk. It has happened.
- **Airbnb splits co-host payouts natively** [ev-23]. That shrinks the split-accounting job to non-Airbnb channels and the true-up.
- **Templates work at the low end.** A spreadsheet template "works well" for a handful of owners [ev-29], so the 5-listing end of the ICP may not pay.
- **Direct add-on competition exists at the same price.** OwnerCenter costs $49/month per manager account [ev-61]. Clearing and VRPlatform target the same job [ev-30, ev-31].
- **Drift and lock issues often get fixed upstream.**
  - Drift is often a one-off misconfiguration, fixed by reconnecting [ev-45].
  - Hospitable shows the price source for each day [ev-46].
  - Airbnb alerts hosts when a lock is offline [ev-56].
  - Hospitable patched its backup-code issue itself [ev-53].

## How it's solved today

- **Direct:**
  - Hospitable: statements, portal, payouts, co-host statements. Payouts are free for the first run each month, then 1.5% capped at $5.
  - Clearing: trust accounting and statements.
  - VRPlatform/VRTrust.
  - OwnerCenter: $49/month add-on.
- **Indirect:**
  - Hostaway: built-in statements. Fees of 1% on payments and 1.8% on booking-engine reservations are reported on top of the subscription [ev-60].
  - OwnerRez: statement views and reports.
  - Guesty.
  - Topkey: STR finance automation.
  - PriceLabs: the pricing source of the drift.
  - Airbnb's native co-host split payouts and lock integration.
- **Manual:** spreadsheet templates fed by PMS or Airbnb exports [ev-62, ev-63, ev-10].
- **Service:** no fetchable evidence on outsourced STR bookkeepers. Accountant job postings show the work is staffed in-house [ev-33].
- **Founder-listed tools with no public evidence collected:** Smoobu, Hostfully and Uplisting. Nothing fetchable showed their owner-statement limits.
