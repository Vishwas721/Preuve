# Research report: Client video review and NLE marker sync for post-production boutiques

Date: 2026-11-06 · Method v3 · Idea `05463b24-7ec4-4474-b6cc-213743391496` · Pack `pack-2026-11-06.json` (86 verified, 0 unverifiable, 0 rejected)

## Bottom line

The core pain is real but narrow. In Adobe Community threads from 2024 to 2026, editors say Frame.io v4 broke the round trip of markers and comments with Premiere and After Effects. Several say they regret the update or are looking for alternatives [ev-01][ev-05][ev-07][ev-10][ev-12].

Against that:
- Adobe has rebuilt the Premiere panel to target this exact problem [ev-13] and has publicly acknowledged the bugs [ev-04].
- Resolve already has its own marker sync through Blackmagic Cloud [ev-27][ev-29].
- Competing tools with editing-software integrations sell for about $10 a seat [ev-34][ev-35].
- Frame.io Team costs $25 a seat and already includes restricted folders [ev-23].

Most of the wedge beyond marker sync has little or no public support: links on the agency's own domain to get past client firewalls, the presentation mode, and the $29 seat price. The evidence supports a free plugin as a wedge. It is weak for a paid platform.

## What supports the problem

- **Marker sync broke in v4 (Premiere).**
  - Sequence markers no longer upload, and there is no manual sync [ev-01].
  - Markers get overwritten or deleted [ev-05].
  - The option to export markers as comments is gone [ev-06].
  - Frame.io support reportedly said importing notes was "removed from v4 for now" [ev-08].
  - Other v3 functions are lost [ev-15].
- **It hurts client workflows.** One editor had got almost every client to give notes in Frame.io and is now back to printouts [ev-07]. Another says the integration was "a huge selling point and time saver" and regrets updating [ev-03].
- **The editing-software integration drives which tool a team picks.** One team moved from Wipster to Frame.io for its Premiere panel, which then stopped working after v4 [ev-09].
- **It is recent and not fully fixed.**
  - In After Effects 2026, "Window > Frame.io V4 does absolutely nothing" [ev-10].
  - After the 25.6.1 rebuild, one user still can't use timeline markers and asks for alternatives [ev-12].
- **There is intent to switch.**
  - Users talk about having to find other software [ev-14][ev-08].
  - A Krock.io testimonial describes replacing Frame.io [ev-36].
  - One editor built a review tool (RevCut) because two big clients refused the large platforms [ev-33].
- **Frame.io has a Resolve gap.** It has no native integration for current Resolve versions [ev-25].
- **People patch it by hand.** Workarounds are home-made scripts that turn comment CSV files into markers [ev-11], or printed notes [ev-07].
- **Network blocking: only weak, adjacent signals.**
  - An Adobe-hosted review link failed only on a corporate network [ev-18].
  - One organisation can't use Frame.io's shortened links for security reasons [ev-19].
  - Uploads stall behind a firewall [ev-20].

## What argues against it

- **Adobe is fixing it.** The panel was rebuilt "from the ground up" in Premiere 25.6, with comments tied to sequence markers [ev-13]. The integrations product manager acknowledged the issues publicly [ev-04].
- **The marker problem predates v4.** Exporting markers as comments already failed on Premiere 23.3 [ev-16]. The v3 panel was praised for importing comments as markers [ev-17].
- **Resolve already has native sync.** Blackmagic Cloud Presentations put reviewer markers straight into the Resolve timeline [ev-27][ev-29]. Reviewers need no account, and a free tier allows 20 presentations [ev-28]. PageProof and RevCut also ship Resolve plugins [ev-30][ev-31].
- **The wedge features already exist elsewhere.**
  - Frame.io client links need no login [ev-24].
  - v4 has a branded Share Builder for client presentations [ev-21].
  - Frame.io Team includes restricted projects and folders [ev-23].
  - Vimeo syncs comments into Premiere and uses links that need no account [ev-39].
  - Unlimited free reviewers are standard [ev-37][ev-35].
- **Prices are lower.**

  | Tool | Price |
  |---|---|
  | Frame.io | $15–25 per member per month [ev-23]; a small tier comes with some Creative Cloud plans [ev-26][ev-25] |
  | Dropbox Replay | $10–12 per user per month [ev-34] |
  | Krock.io | $10 per user per month [ev-35] |
  | RevCut | €29 per month for four seats [ev-32] |

- **The incumbent is large.** Frame.io reported 4 million users at the v4 launch [ev-22].

  - Fallbacks are printouts and CSV-to-marker scripts [ev-07][ev-11].
  - Resolve users have native Blackmagic sync. Vimeo, Replay, PageProof and RevCut also offer editing-software integrations [ev-29][ev-39][ev-34][ev-30][ev-31].
  - How common each method is: not found.
- **How often does client IT block review links?** Partially answered.
  - Only adjacent cases were found [ev-18][ev-19][ev-20], plus clients refusing the big platforms over GDPR and AI-training terms [ev-33].
  - How often it happens: no evidence.
- **Editor time lost to missing or misaligned markers.** No evidence found. Editors describe the problem as crippling [ev-10][ev-07], but nobody quantifies it.
- **What teams use to replace Frame.io v4 features.** Answered.
  - Workarounds: printed notes and home-made scripts.
  - Alternatives: Replay, Krock.io, Wipster, Vimeo, PageProof, RevCut and Blackmagic Cloud.
  - Adobe's rebuild is ongoing [ev-13][ev-12].
  - Frame.io already covers restricted folders, no-login links and branded presentations [ev-23][ev-24][ev-21].
- **Who makes the buying decision?** No evidence found. The prospects' contact pages list executive producers and owners, but nothing about purchasing.
- **Per-seat budget.** Partially answered.
  - Market prices are $10–25 per seat [ev-23][ev-34][ev-35][ev-32], with free or bundled tiers [ev-26][ev-28].
  - There is no evidence that boutiques would pay $29 a seat.
  - Zero-egress storage, such as Cloudflare R2, limits the cost of serving video playback [ev-40].

## How it's solved today

- **Direct**
  - Frame.io, the incumbent: Premiere and After Effects panels, $15 or $25 per member [ev-23][ev-24].
  - Dropbox Replay: $10–12 per user; integrates with Premiere, After Effects, Final Cut Pro and Resolve [ev-34].
  - Krock.io: $10 per user, with custom branding [ev-35].
  - Wipster: unlimited reviewers, Premiere and After Effects panels [ev-37][ev-38].
  - PageProof: plugins for Resolve Studio, Premiere, After Effects and Final Cut Pro [ev-30].
  - RevCut: Resolve marker plugin, €5–149 per month [ev-31][ev-32].
- **Indirect**
  - Blackmagic Cloud Presentations [ev-27][ev-29].
  - Vimeo Review [ev-39].
- **Manual:** printed notes [ev-07] and home-made CSV-to-marker scripts [ev-11].
- **Service:** none found.
- **Competitors you listed:** public evidence was found for all of them except Air. Air appears only as the author of a comparison article [ev-26]; its own review product isn't cited.
