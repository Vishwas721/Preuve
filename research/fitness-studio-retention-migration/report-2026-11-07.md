## Bottom line

The pains are real. Studios do lose members who quietly stop coming, and getting off Mindbody is slow, with card-data release as the hardest step. Both wedges, though, run into competition that already exists and is often free. The incumbents now ship lapse detection and win-back automations: Mindbody (including churn prediction at its new $79 entry price), PushPress and WellnessLiving. Every competing platform, Mindbody included, advertises free migration. The evidence supports a much narrower bet than the idea describes: a cheap win-back layer for studios on lower Mindbody tiers that don't have the Ultimate automations. Even there, an illustrative vendor model puts recovered revenue at only about $200–$400 a month for a 300-member studio [ev-37], so a $49–$99/month price is tight. The migration-service wedge (b) is weak.

## What supports the problem

- **Leaving Mindbody involves real friction.** The card-number (PAN) transfer needs a formal release request that takes 3–4 weeks [ev-14]. A fuller export is a paid service, available for only 30 days after cancellation [ev-12]. One operator moved data by hand rather than pay a $499 export fee [ev-05]. Exporters can time out on large data [ev-17], and basic plans may lack the report needed to move class-pack balances [ev-18]. Card migrations can fail; one report says nearly 300 clients' payment data did not transfer cleanly [ev-07]. Some migration paths have the studio collect members' cards again by phone or in person [ev-19].
- **Contracts auto-renew.** Terms run 12, 24 or 36 months and renew automatically unless notice is given [ev-10], so the renewal date is a predictable decision point.
- **Price rises, add-on fees and ownership changes are cited as reasons to switch** [ev-01, ev-02, ev-03, ev-04, ev-09, ev-22, ev-51, ev-52]. Most of this comes from competitors' marketing or secondhand Reddit quotes.
- **Retention pressure is real.** Average annual member retention is 66%, according to the HFA 2025 benchmark as cited by WellnessLiving [ev-32]. 61% of US studio owners say members find memberships less affordable [ev-46]. Mindbody's own training says chasing every lapsed client by hand "isn't feasible" [ev-29].
- **Mindbody's full automated email/SMS campaigns sit in its top (Ultimate) tier** [ev-28]. That leaves lower-tier studios without them.
- **Owners and desk staff do retention by hand.** Personalized outreach is the top retention approach (56%) [ev-42]. A Pilates front-desk job lists "retain clients" alongside CRM work with the owner-operator [ev-50]. Studios reportedly pull data into Google Sheets weekly [ev-08], though this is secondhand.

## What argues against it

- **Incumbents already automate win-back.** Mindbody has a "45 Day reengagement" automation [ev-29] and churn-prediction models in its $79/month entry plan [ev-27]. PushPress flags at-risk members every morning [ev-30]. WellnessLiving launched win-back automations in April 2026 [ev-31].
- **Add-on vendors already fill the gap on Mindbody:** FitGrid [ev-33], DailyStory [ev-36], Carly (waitlist fill, first-visit and lapse follow-up) [ev-39] and APIANT's Mindbody-to-Keap 21-day win-back [ev-38].
- **Migration is given away free.** Arketa [ev-21], Walla (usually including cards) [ev-23], bsport [ev-25] and Mindbody itself [ev-24] all advertise it. A small studio reports a complete Mindbody-to-Momence transfer of memberships, packs and future bookings [ev-26]. On Stripe-based platforms such as Glofox, card transfer may not be needed at all [ev-15].
- **Some premises are weaker than assumed.** Month-to-month Mindbody plans exist after a 90-day minimum [ev-11]. Mindbody's export file is called the "cleanest" by a rival platform's co-founder [ev-06]. Mindbody's entry price is now $79/month [ev-27].
- **Small value per studio.** An illustrative model recovers 1–2 members a month, about $200–$400 [ev-37].
- **Studios are wary of new tools.** 57% of owners say new technology creates operational challenges [ev-45].
- **The API costs money and has gates.** Mindbody's API is metered per call, needs manual approval, and each site must be activated by its owner [ev-40, ev-41].

## How it's solved today

- **Indirect (all-in-one platforms):**
  - Mindbody: 45-day re-engagement automation, churn prediction, full campaigns in Ultimate, free migration in [ev-24, ev-27, ev-28, ev-29].
  - WellnessLiving: Marketing Suite [ev-31].
  - PushPress: at-risk workflow [ev-30].
  - Arketa: free migration, bundled automations [ev-21, ev-22].
  - Walla [ev-23], bsport [ev-25], Momence [ev-26], TeamUp [ev-48], Zipper [ev-14, ev-16].
- **Direct (retention add-ons):** FitGrid [ev-33], DailyStory [ev-36], Carly [ev-39], APIANT CRMConnect + Keap [ev-38].
- **Manual:** personal check-in emails and weekly spreadsheet pulls [ev-08, ev-42, ev-48].
- **Service:** migration is mostly done free by the destination vendor's onboarding team. A Fiverr migration gig turned up in search but could not be fetched.
- **Founder-listed competitors with no first-hand evidence here:**
