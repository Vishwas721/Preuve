# Swiggy/Zomato payout audit for Indian restaurants and cloud kitchens: research report (2026-11-05)

Idea: `639c2ad4-b6e1-4a60-9667-fe45b2192591` · Method v3 · Pack: `pack-2026-11-05.json`
Import result: 65 verified, 0 unverifiable, 0 rejected.

## Bottom line

The pain is real and current, but it isn't quite the pain the idea describes. The strongest 2025-2026 evidence is about
**unconsented ad, discount and fee deductions** and slow, painful disputes. Bengaluru associations threatened a boycott,
and chains report losses of Rs 13-16 lakh. Arithmetic errors between orders and payouts are described mostly by vendors
who sell reconciliation, and nobody has measured how often they happen. Order-level reconciliation is already bundled free
into the leading POS (Petpooja). A WhatsApp-based AI competitor (SideKyk) is pitching the same wedge. One estimate also
suggests the money recoverable from pure discrepancies per outlet is small. A deduction-and-consent audit with dispute
recovery priced as a share of recoveries looks more promising than a plain order matcher.

## What supports the problem

- **Large unconsented deductions are widely reported.**
  - A six-outlet chain found Rs 16 lakh deducted in small amounts without notice [ev-28].
  - A chain took nearly two years to recover Rs 10 lakh from Swiggy [ev-39].
  - Approved ads auto-renew, and the deductions go unnoticed until payout [ev-34].
  - "Every month, something goes wrong" [ev-36].
  - A 16-outlet group lost Rs 1.4 lakh to unapproved membership fees [ev-37].
  - NRAI's Bengaluru chapter reports unexplained deductions and delayed or missing payments [ev-40].
- **Associations are demanding this.** They want itemised settlement reports and relationship managers to resolve payment
  discrepancies [ev-26], and transparency in payouts [ev-25].
- **Self-audits find money.** Glisco Kitchens found mis-assigned Swiggy ad charges during an audit, and Swiggy admitted a
  backend error [ev-35].
- **Pricing as a share of recoveries has precedent.** GetMyRefund takes 10% of what it recovers and got back about 70% of
  one chain's claim [ev-29].
- **Manual effort (vendor figures).**
  - 2-3 h/week at 80+ orders/day [ev-10].
  - 3-5 h/week [ev-01].
  - Half a day a week for a 19-outlet chain [ev-02].
  - The effort grows with outlets, platforms and brands per kitchen [ev-07].
- **Disputes are painful.** Owners describe "an endlessly long ordeal" that many abandon [ev-38]. One complaint was
  blocked by a dashboard error [ev-30].

## What argues against it

- **Incumbents bundle it free.** Petpooja POSS includes reconciliation at no extra cost [ev-14]. One customer chose
  UrbanPiper because it already held their POS order data [ev-04].
- **Same-wedge competitor.** SideKyk is building WhatsApp AI agents for settlement audits; it is waitlist only for now
  [ev-47].
- **Errors may be rare or small.**
  - A Swiggy-side source says under 1% of partners are affected, with complaints resolved within 24 hours [ev-32].
  - Zomato says its consent system gives partners control [ev-33].
  - Petpooja says some owners find everything lines up [ev-11], and that differences are small below 30-40 orders a day
    [ev-12].
  - One estimate puts unrecovered discrepancies at Rs 5,000-15,000 a year on Rs 10 lakh/month of revenue, which is less
    than the proposed Rs 799-1,999 per outlet per month [ev-19].
- **The losses are mostly a different kind.** The headline cases are unconsented charges, not order mismatches [ev-23].
- **The market is shifting.** Swiggy agreed to refunds after collective pressure [ev-41]. Zero-commission entrants such
  as Rapido's Ownly are courting restaurants [ev-42].
