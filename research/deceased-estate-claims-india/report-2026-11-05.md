# Deceased-estate asset tracing and claim orchestration (India, incl. NRI families): research report

## Bottom line

The problem is real and well documented. Heirs, NRIs especially, lose months to succession
certificates, branch visits and per-institution paperwork, and they routinely miss assets they never knew
existed [ev-01, ev-08, ev-19, ev-20]. But two premises of the idea are weak. First, **dedicated products
already exist**: FamEase, EasyInherit's "submission-ready claim dockets", Yellow Smart, Share Samadhan and
Fundtracers [ev-38, ev-41, ev-44, ev-35, ev-33]. Second, **regulators are actively removing the pain**:
there is a unified unclaimed-assets portal in the works, RBI's three-document nominee claims, IBA's common
form, SEBI's 21-day transmission limit, and bank incentives to settle claims [ev-10, ev-12, ev-26, ev-11,
ev-13, ev-06]. What's left is the hard tail: no nominee or no will, overseas heirs, and branch-level refusals.
Whether families will pay for that is untested, because there is no public evidence on who decides to buy.

## What supports the problem

- **Cost and time of the no-will route.** A succession certificate is estimated at Rs 10–20 lakh total cost
  and 6–18 months [ev-01], or 4–7 months when uncontested [ev-32]. Fewer than 10% of Indians make a formal
  will [ev-46].
- **No single place to look.** Unclaimed assets sit with RBI, IEPFA, EPFO, insurers and AMCs with "no unified
  registry" [ev-07]. UDGAM only locates deposits and doesn't process claims [ev-05]. About Rs 98,073 crore
  sits in the DEA Fund [ev-03].
- **Heirs don't know what exists.** Heirs may not know an account or FD exists [ev-04]. One family found an
  EPF account only via an old payslip and found an unknown co-op bank account by chance [ev-20]. A commenter
  says discovering unknown accounts is "the worst part" [ev-22].
- **Lived pain.** Four SBI branch visits, each turned away for a different missing document [ev-19]. Two
  months of unpaid leave [ev-21]. A year of back-and-forth despite being the nominee [ev-16]. A death in 2020
  with the claim still processing in 2025 [ev-17].
- **NRIs specifically.** Apostilled documents, POAs and multiple affidavits are needed [ev-02]. "The harder
  part for NRIs is rarely the court. It is assembling the document set across time zones and family members"
  [ev-31]. A non-resident heir with Rs 50 lakh+ in un-nominated MFs asks a forum how to start [ev-15].
- **Decentralised claims discourage recovery.** Per-institution processing "often delays or discourages
  recovery" [ev-08].

## What argues against it

- **Competitors already do this.** FamEase sells a tracked workspace for account claims and institutional
  submissions, aimed at NRIs [ev-38, ev-39]. EasyInherit's EasyClaims "creates submission-ready claim dockets"
  [ev-41]. Yellow Smart offers post-death support [ev-44]. Share Samadhan traces unknown investments for NRIs
  "with minimal information" and no India visit [ev-36]. The idea's "no dedicated cross-institution product"
  claim does not hold.
- **The free alternatives are improving fast.** A single integrated government portal for all unclaimed
  assets is being built [ev-10, ev-12]. The 2025 "Your Money, Your Right" camps returned Rs 5,777 crore across
  22.95 lakh claims for free [ev-09]. A CAS on the deceased's PAN surfaces MF and demat holdings [ev-27, ev-30].
- **The claims themselves are getting simpler.** A nominee now gets a bank balance against three documents
  [ev-26]. IBA has a common application form [ev-11]. SEBI proposes 21-day processing and online tracking
  [ev-13]. Banks earn 5–7.5% per settled unclaimed claim, so they have their own reason to chase claimants
  [ev-06].
- **Nominations remove most of the pain.** "Nominees, wills and trusts are the way to go" [ev-23]. A
  UAE-based daughter completed MF transmission "smooth, and reasonably quick" where nominees existed [ev-47].
  An NRI guide says presence is needed "less than families fear" [ev-25].
- **Free escalation works for some.** The RBI Ombudsman resolved death-claim grievances [ev-18].

## How it's solved today

- **Direct:** FamEase [ev-38, ev-39], EasyInherit / EasyClaims [ev-41, ev-42] and Yellow Smart [ev-44, ev-45].
- **Service:** Share Samadhan [ev-35–ev-37], Fundtracers [ev-33, ev-34], and local lawyers and CAs [ev-43,
  ev-28, ev-55, ev-64].
- **Indirect:** the UDGAM, MITRA and Bima Bharosa portals and the planned unified portal [ev-05, ev-10,
  ev-12, ev-09], plus Surakshakadi's asset-transfer service [ev-40].
- **Manual:** a CAS from CAMS, KFintech, NSDL or CDSL [ev-27, ev-30]; branch visits, POAs and the RBI
  Ombudsman [ev-19, ev-29, ev-18].
- **Founder-listed adjacent players:** RegisterKaro and LegalKart were **not researched** in this pass, and
  no evidence about them is in the pack. No public pricing was found for any competitor.
