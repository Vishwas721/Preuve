## Bottom line

The problem is already being solved at every layer:

- by the dominant platform, Procore, which ships AI log and summary agents [ev-15, ev-18, ev-20];
- by point apps for photos, daily reports and visual capture: CompanyCam, Raken, Constructable,
  OpenSpace [ev-23, ev-24, ev-28, ev-17];
- by spreadsheets and free templates [ev-14, ev-29];
- on the lender side, by paid third-party monitoring firms [ev-30, ev-32].

**A generic "AI progress report from photos" product would be a late entrant into a crowded
space.** The least-served piece the evidence points to is still assembling owner- and
lender-specific reports. That's an inference to test in interviews, not a finding.

| Service | Lender monitoring firms (Partner ESI, RLB) | Monthly site visits and independent progress reports for lenders | ev-30, ev-31, ev-32 |

## What customers say

The only complaint about Procore's daily log that Preuve could verify comes from a competitor:
"click-heavy and difficult to navigate… for superintendents and field staff" [ev-27]. A Raken
executive makes a similar claim about complex suites [ev-26]. Both are biased sources.

Independent reviews (G2, Capterra, TrustRadius) block Preuve's crawler, so **there is no
verified independent sentiment yet.** That's the open unknown on how well Procore's AI agents
work, and it needs interviews with Procore users.
