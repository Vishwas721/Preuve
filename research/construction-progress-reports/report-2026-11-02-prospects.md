# Research report: Construction progress automation (prospects)

- **Idea:** `9f259df8-99c5-4f88-b3b1-e1d57cecbb66`
- **Method:** `/research-idea` v3, step 5c (prospecting)
- **Target ICP:** Mid-sized Commercial General Contractors (50–250 employees). Users: Project
  Engineer, PM, Field Engineer. Buyers: VP of Construction Operations, Director of Operations,
  CFO.
- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Verification:** 31 of 31 quotes verified; 22 companies; 3 contacts

## Bottom line

22 US general contractors are now prospects. Every fact comes from a quote on the company's own
public site that Preuve verified. **Scores are low, top 46/100, and that's the honest result.**
Almost no company publicly shows *evidence of the problem*, the largest component at 25 points.
Only Brinkmann does, through its documented weekly owner-report process [ev-01, ev-02]. Size is
stated by just 3 firms, and nobody publishes their software stack.

The list is a starting point for **interview outreach (Phase 2–3)**, not proof of demand.

## What would raise scores honestly

- **Problem evidence per company:** job postings that describe weekly or monthly owner and
  lender reporting, or project engineer duties. Most job boards and career sites blocked Preuve's
  crawler in this run.
- **Contacts in buyer or user roles:** leadership pages with named Directors of Operations,
  CFOs or project engineers.
- **Stated headcounts:** careers or about pages.

## Method notes and limits

- **About 30 more candidates were dropped:** their sites returned 403, robots.txt blocks, TLS
  certificate-chain errors or DNS failures, or pages with no usable text. Fetchability, not fit,
  shaped part of this list.
- **Contacts:** 3, each a name printed next to a title in a verified quote from the company's
  own page. No emails were collected; none appeared next to a person.
- **Assessments:** company fit is rated 4/5 for regional commercial GCs and 1–2/5 for national
  firms. Technology fit and other signals are 0 for all: no evidence.
