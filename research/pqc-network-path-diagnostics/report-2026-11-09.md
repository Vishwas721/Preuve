# Research report: Post-quantum crypto migration, network-path diagnostics and crypto inventory (CBOM)

Idea id: `1ffaf3a2-88d8-4c18-a2fd-f86e936bc7a1` · Method v3 · 2026-11-09 · Pack: `pack-2026-11-09.json`
Import result (final): 78 verified, 0 unverifiable, 0 rejected.

## Bottom line

The technical problem is real and well documented. Hybrid PQC ClientHellos have broken TLS-inspecting firewalls, IPS engines and SNI-sniffing proxies [ev-03][ev-04][ev-05][ev-11][ev-17], and inspecting firewalls can silently strip PQC on decrypted hops [ev-18]. But the evidence also shows this breakage is a shrinking tail. Vendors patched it [ev-07], Cloudflare reports 99.2% of PQ origin handshakes completing cleanly [ev-09], and free tools already test external endpoints [ev-08][ev-47].

The inventory/CBOM half has firm compliance drivers (PCI DSS 12.3.3, NIST IR 8547, the EU roadmap, RBI Q-SAFE) [ev-30][ev-24][ev-26][ev-27]. It is also a crowded space with well-funded incumbents and open-source options [ev-49]–[ev-53]. The most defensible angle the evidence points to is internal/east-west paths and proving "PQ actually negotiated vs. silent fallback". No buyer has been heard saying they would pay for it, and budget signals are weak.

## What supports the problem

- **Chrome's rollout broke middleboxes.** Google says enabling hybrid Kyber "revealed a number of previously-existing bugs in several TLS middlebox products" [ev-03]. Admins reported firewalls from Fortinet, SonicWall, Palo Alto Networks and AWS dropping connections [ev-04].
- **Concrete outage mode.** On FortiGate flow-based deep inspection, "certain websites will fail to load" [ev-05]. A consultancy calls TLS inspection appliances rejecting the hybrid group "the single most common production issue" [ev-11]. A reverse proxy misroutes because the SNI lands in the second TCP packet [ev-17].
- **Long tail inside networks.** An IETF participant focused on internal telecom networks expects PQ-blocking middleboxes to "still be around in 2030–2035" [ev-15]. "Middleboxes and load-balancers is the big problem" [ev-16].
- **Silent downgrade on inspected paths.** Palo Alto NGFWs strip PQC from the ClientHello on decrypted sessions, forcing classical crypto [ev-18]. Consul guidance: "Do not report fallback connections as post-quantum protected" [ev-21]. CDN PQC does not extend to "internal APIs, service-to-service communications" [ev-22].
- **Diagnosis is manual today.** The vendor diagnosis is a packet capture in Wireshark [ev-06]. Admins are told to contact the vendor [ev-02] or "audit every device terminating or inspecting TLS" [ev-10].
- **Compliance drivers.**
  - NIST IR 8547 final deprecates all quantum-vulnerable public-key algorithms after 2030 [ev-24].
  - The EU wants critical infrastructure moved by end-2030 [ev-26].
  - PCI DSS 12.3.3 requires a cipher-suite inventory [ev-30].
  - RBI's Q-SAFE committee mandate includes a CBOM-based sector audit [ev-27][ev-28].
  - CERT-In covers CBOM [ev-29].
- **Large banks run PQC programs.** Citi has an enterprise-wide Post Quantum Readiness Program and notes "increasing frequency of internal and external regulatory requests" [ev-44][ev-45].

## What argues against it

- **Breakage is being fixed by vendors.** Fortinet shipped fixed IPS engines [ev-07], which is the risk the idea itself names.
- **At scale it mostly works.** Cloudflare: 99.2% of PQ origin connections complete in one round trip [ev-09]. "For most connections it is completely invisible" [ev-12].
- **Free tools cover the external check.**
  - Cloudflare Radar tests hostnames for PQ TLS bugs and explains failures [ev-08].
  - pqcscan is free and open source [ev-47].
  - CBOMkit generates CBOMs for free [ev-53].
- **Low priority and weak budgets.**
  - ISACA 2025: only 5% call quantum a high near-term priority, and 41% have no plans [ev-37][ev-38].
  - "Most companies shouldn't be focusing on it over the rest of their security priorities" [ev-39].
  - "A migration nobody has put a number on is a migration nobody will fund" [ev-40].
  - BFSI "funding for quantum prep remains low" [ev-42].
- **Deadlines are soft for private firms.** For commercial organizations NIST deprecation "creates a documented risk-acceptance obligation" rather than a ban [ev-25]. RBI has a committee, not a mandate [ev-27], and the CBOM is "moving … toward an expected" capability [ev-35].
- **Crowded inventory market.** Keyfactor AgileSec (including network-traffic sensors), SandboxAQ, IBM and Encryption Consulting already sell discovery and CBOM [ev-49]–[ev-52][ev-54].
