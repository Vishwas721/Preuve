# Research report: Commercial policy checking and carrier-statement reconciliation sidecar for independent insurance agencies

Idea id: `a39be586-0442-41b3-900c-b0357098097e` · Method v3 · 2026-11-10
Import: 89 verified, 0 unverifiable, 0 rejected (89 evidence items) · 13 competitors · 21 companies · 0 contacts

## Bottom line

The problem is real and agencies already pay for it. Policy checking is manual, inconsistent and often outsourced, and the outsourced checks are slow and miss errors [ev-07, ev-10, ev-50, ev-21]. Agency-bill statements were matched by hand until very recently [ev-26]. But the timing is poor. In October 2026 Applied (Epic Conductor, Applied Recon) and Vertafore (Policy Check and Intake agents) shipped native versions of all three parts of the wedge [ev-33, ev-34, ev-35, ev-27, ev-43, ev-44]. Funded startups already sell source-linked checking with AMS write-back [ev-15, ev-14], and Applied has shown it will cut off a third-party tool that connects to Epic [ev-45, ev-46]. The remaining opening is narrow: complex commercial and E&S books, accuracy you can audit, and possibly Vertafore or smaller-AMS shops [ev-37, ev-52, ev-38]. Treat this as an idea that needs a sharp niche, not a broad product.

## What supports the problem

- **Manual and inconsistent checking.**
  - Agencies compare 100+ page documents against each other and against AMS data "using only their eyes as tools" [ev-20].
  - Some agencies let each account manager decide how thoroughly to check [ev-10].
  - Checking is often handed to junior staff, and backlogs are frequent [ev-07]. Renewal-season backlogs have run up to six months [ev-02].
- **Outsourcing is slow and misses errors.**
  - At one brokerage, checks "shipped off and returned in two weeks, sometimes longer," and an outsourced check missed an error that stayed uncorrected for a policy year [ev-50].
  - The old process was a 14-day wait, then about an hour of manual Epic updates [ev-51].
  - Offshore outsourcing "still took a significant time and generated backlogs, and more importantly, errors persisted" [ev-04].
- **A budget line exists.**
  - Many agencies outsource checking to Patra at a per-policy price [ev-21].
  - India-based outsourcers handle high-volume checking with staff trained on Epic [ev-17]. Offshore pricing models include per policy [ev-19].
  - A large brokerage replaced part of its outsourced service with software [ev-06].
  - A Rhode Island brokerage's account managers work with Patra on policy-checking discrepancies [ev-64].
- **Re-keying policies that don't download.**
  - Vertafore confirms that when policies aren't available by carrier download, agencies enter them by hand [ev-41]. One agency says "Most of our farm policies don't download" [ev-42].
  - Commercial-heavy agencies re-key data more often than personal-lines agencies, 70% vs 62% (a submissions survey) [ev-40].
- **Reconciliation breaks on configuration.**
  - A wrong premium-payable setup on the policy blocks agency-bill reconciliation [ev-23]. Mismatches stop accounting and go back to servicing staff [ev-22].
  - Applied itself said agency bill "has stayed the manual exception," matched by hand in spreadsheets [ev-26]. Statements outside Ivans are keyed in policy by policy [ev-30].
  - INSURICA pays a $60k–$65k specialist to reconcile carrier statements against Epic [ev-28, ev-29].
- **Accuracy fear is documented.**
  - Trade press questions Applied's unexplained "99% accuracy"; 1% field errors could mean wrong limits [ev-38].
  - An E&O claim arose because an AI review vendor's checklist left out defense coverage across tower layers [ev-53].
  - A vendor blog: "'mostly right' isn't good enough" [ev-55].
  - A Swiss Re underwriter says policy-review AI "pays for itself" in E&S, where coverage changes daily [ev-52].
- **A possible gap in incumbent coverage.** Agencies "with complex commercial books, spread across regional carriers and wholesale markets, may find more of the work still lands on staff" [ev-37].

## What argues against it

- **Incumbents shipped the wedge in October 2026.**
  - Epic Conductor checks every bound policy against the quote, extracts dec pages into Epic, and automates direct-bill, agency-bill and bank reconciliation [ev-33, ev-35, ev-34]. Applied Recon has live AI agency-bill reconciliation [ev-27].
  - Vertafore's Policy Check Agent (up to 90% less time) and Policy Intake Agent (up to 87% less time on a CPP/GL package) are generally available [ev-43, ev-44].
- **Startups already sell it.**
  - CopyCat flags issues "with the source behind every flag" and opens each finding on the exact page, which is this idea's claimed differentiator [ev-15].
  - Outmarket compares four sources, writes back to Epic and AMS360, raised a $34.5M Series B and claims 300+ agencies [ev-13, ev-14].
  - Others: Exdion (since 2019) [ev-05], Quandri's new commercial policy check-in [ev-16], Fulcrum [ev-51], and Comulate and Ascend for reconciliation [ev-32, ev-31].
- **Some agencies already solved it.** Account managers at large brokerages say AI policy checking replaced their manual check [ev-48].
- **Buyers resist sidecars.**
  - Agents "have less interest in learning another standalone system" [ev-59].
  - "Your team doesn't need another tool" [ev-56].
  - Applied argues tools layered outside the AMS "create more friction" [ev-25].
- **Platform risk.** Applied cut off Comulate's Epic integration; Applied also owns Ivans [ev-45]. The court declined to force the integration back after June 30, 2026 [ev-46].
- **E&O impact may be smaller than assumed.**
  - Failure to procure coverage is the leading E&O cause [ev-47].
  - Manual checking reportedly already catches about 95% of errors [ev-03].
  - Ivans download covers most standard commercial lines when the carrier takes part [ev-61], which shrinks the re-keying problem to E&S, farm and other policies that don't download.

## Method notes

- **Queries:** 23 queries, listed in `meta.queries`. Three were contradiction searches: E&O cause statistics, agencies skipping checks, and AI accuracy distrust.
- **Source mix:** news and trade press (Insurance Business, Insurance Journal, IA Magazine, Risk & Insurance, Leader's Edge), vendor sites, job postings (Paylocity, Vaia) and agency websites.
- **Limits:**
  - Much of the evidence is vendor material or vendor-sponsored, so time-saved and accuracy figures are vendor claims.
  - No Reddit or forum threads were found.
  - Sites that blocked the fetcher, which I avoided citing: Coverager, BeInsure, Insurance Thought Leadership, Upwork, Vertafore.com, Roughnotes, and several agency sites (Higginbotham, Marshall & Sterling, Bolton, IOA).
  - Contacts are left empty by design, and no personal names appear in quotes or statements.
  - The Swiss Re E&O article dates from 2024 and the "failure to procure" item from 2022; the rest are mostly 2026.
