# Research report: No-portal subcontractor COI and endorsement verification for small US contractors

Idea id: `1e5751b0-a185-40b9-a004-972ce52c872d` · Method v3 · 2026-11-05 · Import: 68 verified, 0 unverifiable, 0 rejected.

## Bottom line

The problem is real and recurring. Small and mid-size GCs pay coordinators to collect, check and chase subcontractor COIs. They usually do it in spreadsheets or by keying dates into an ERP. Certificates that lack the actual additional-insured endorsement have cost GCs over $1M in court.

But the proposed wedge is already taken. bcs is free up to 25 vendors and then costs $0.95/vendor/month. TrustLayer is free under 50 vendors. bcs, Billy and Jones already offer no-login vendor submission and AI endorsement review. At least four indie tools (CertKeeper, COIPulse, VendorShield, CertTrack Lite) sell at $29–49/month, and one of them uses the same "no sub portal" pitch.

The evidence supports the pain. It argues against "$99/month, no portal" being a differentiated position.

## What supports the problem

- **The manual work is paid for.** Contractor job postings list COI collection, expiry tracking and contract-compliance checks as coordinator duties [ev-01, ev-02, ev-03, ev-04, ev-05]. One of these roles pays $55k–60k [ev-02]. Another pairs COIs with "tracking spreadsheets" [ev-04].
- **Endorsements matter, and certificates alone fail.**
  - A 2026 Arkansas appeals ruling left a GC uncovered because it had no additional-insured endorsement; the loss exceeded $1.2M [ev-33, ev-34].
  - In a California case, a GC listed only as certificate holder had no coverage [ev-35].
- **Audits force the issue.** At a workers' comp premium audit, a missing sub COI can add that sub's whole cost to the contractor's payroll [ev-39]. Carriers ask for COIs at audits, claims and loss-control inspections [ev-40].
- **Incumbent minimums are real.**
  - CertFocus: $7,500 self-service and $10,000 full-service minimums, plus a $3,500–4,800 implementation fee [ev-14, ev-15].
  - bcs full-service: $10,000 minimum [ev-11].
  - myCOI: sales-led only [ev-25].
  - A small property manager was quoted $8.4k–12k/year and stayed on spreadsheets [ev-08].
- **Brokers resent extra steps.**
  - Issuing COIs is unpaid broker work [ev-37].
  - One contractor's system charged subs' agents $15 per upload [ev-38].
  - Trade-press estimates say about half of initial construction COI submissions are rejected [ev-36]. That supports the value of a deficiency email.
- **A regional GC shows the workaround.** Its Controller asked peers about COI practice; the firm tracks COI dates manually in CMiC [ev-06, ev-07].

## What argues against it

- **Cheap and free self-serve tools already exist.**
  - bcs is free to 25 vendors, then $0.95/vendor/month with no minimum, about $71/month at 75 vendors [ev-10].
  - bcs's free tier already includes extraction, automated requests and deficiency notices [ev-12].
  - TrustLayer is free under 50 vendors [ev-24].
- **"No portal" is not unique.** bcs [ev-21], Billy [ev-22] and Jones [ev-23] offer no-login submission links. Billy and Jones also market AI endorsement review [ev-22, ev-23]. CertKeeper pitches exactly "without forcing your subs into a new portal" [ev-26].
- **Indie tools undercut $99.** COIPulse charges $49/month for 50–150 vendors [ev-09], VendorShield $49/month flat [ev-27] and CertTrack Lite $29/month [ev-29].
- **The "subs hate portals" premise is mixed.** One source says vendors *want* an upload link instead of emailing PDFs [ev-42]. Chasing by email alone produced 3-week chains [ev-41].
- **Very small GCs may not need it.** Even a vendor says a spreadsheet is adequate at about 8 subs and only becomes a bottleneck around 30 [ev-30].
- **Generic AI extraction into Excel is pitched as a substitute** [ev-32].

## How it's solved today

- **Direct:**
  - Established tools: bcs, CertFocus (Vertikal RMS), myCOI/illumend, TrustLayer, Jones, Billy and SmartCompliance. SmartCompliance's only evidence is a competitor's table [ev-17].
  - Indie tools: CertKeeper (pre-launch, no-portal), COIPulse, VendorShield and CertTrack Lite.
- **Indirect:** NetVendor (vendor compliance for property managers), CMiC and other construction ERPs, and generic AI extraction to a spreadsheet (ImageToTable).
- **Manual:** spreadsheets, shared drives and email, run by project coordinators.
- **Service:** full-service outsourcing from bcs and CertFocus, with $10k minimums.
- **Founder-listed, no first-party evidence:** CertifiKit's domain did not resolve, and SmartCompliance's own site timed out.

## Method notes

- **Queries:** 14 evidence queries plus regional prospect searches. At least 2 were contradiction searches (spreadsheets suffice; cheap or free alternatives).
- **Sources used:** job postings, a CFMA forum post, trade press (IA Magazine, Insurance Journal, Insurance Business), carrier pages (State Farm, Falls Lake) and vendor/competitor pages.
- **Blocked sources:** G2, Capterra, TrustRadius, Software Advice, Reddit, BiggerPockets, ContractorTalk and the InsuranceJournal forums. As a result there are **no first-hand user reviews** of incumbents.
- **Claims from competitors:** the portal-friction and review-delay claims about myCOI and CertFocus come from a competitor (Billy).
- **Vendor figures:** many hour and cost numbers are vendor marketing and are marked `inference`.
- **Unverified:** the idea's claim of "review delays of up to 2 weeks" was not verified.
